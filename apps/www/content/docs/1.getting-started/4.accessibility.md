---

## Reduced motion

Every goopUI component honors `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. It doesn't drop the goo. It switches to a still version:

- **Kept:** the goo body shape and any resting drips. A reduced-motion button still looks like a goopUI button.
- **Turned off:** the drip simulation, wobble, squish, and pointer-follow.
- **State changes:** instant, or a short fade.

The engine watches the setting live. If someone turns it on while the page is open, running drips clear and the body snaps back to rest.

You can also force it from your app by setting an attribute on `<html>`. The engine watches that attribute as well:

```ts
document.documentElement.dataset.reducedMotion = 'true'
```

That's what the switch below does. It also turns off this site's smooth scrolling and scroll reveals.

:reduced-motion-demo

Per element, `attachGoop(el, { reducedMotion: true })` forces the still mode and `false` ignores the setting. The default, `'auto'`, follows the OS setting and the attribute.

## Assistive tech

- The goo layer has `aria-hidden="true"` and `pointer-events: none`. Screen readers, hit-testing, and focus only ever see your real element.
- Labels and icons sit above the goo layer, unfiltered, so text stays sharp and keeps its contrast.
- Roles, keyboard handling, and focus management come from reka-ui. They're the same primitives shadcn-vue uses.
- Focus styles come from your theme. The goo doesn't replace or cover your focus ring.

## Touch and pointers

- Hover drips only run when `(hover: hover)` matches and the pointer is a mouse. A tap on a touch screen never leaves drips running.
- Touch and pen get a squish on press and one drip per tap.
- A right click doesn't squish.
- Disabled components ignore interaction. Drips already running finish, and nothing new starts.

## Layout

Drips never change layout. The goo layer is absolutely positioned and padded outside the element, so content around a dripping button doesn't move.
