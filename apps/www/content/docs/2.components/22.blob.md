## Planned behavior

- Large goo shapes that move slowly, for hero sections, empty states, and backgrounds.
- **Reduced motion:** a still shape.

## Why it's last

Blob is the stress test. Big goo areas are the most expensive thing for an SVG filter to draw, especially in Safari. The engine already hides its renderer behind an interface, so if the SVG filter can't keep up at Blob's sizes, a canvas metaball renderer can be added without changing any component. Blob is what decides whether that's needed.
