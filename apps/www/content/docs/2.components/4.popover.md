GoopPopover is fourth in the v0.1 build order. The behavior below is the plan.
::

## Planned behavior

- **Open:** a goo bridge stretches from the trigger to the popover and pinches off in about 250ms. Then the filter turns off so the content is crisp.
- **Close:** the popover shrinks back toward the trigger, faster than it opened.
- **Content stays sharp.** The goo is only there during the transition. Your content is never filtered.
- **Reduced motion:** no bridge. The popover appears instantly or with a short fade.

It's built on reka-ui's `Popover`, so focus handling, dismissal, and positioning work like shadcn-vue's.
