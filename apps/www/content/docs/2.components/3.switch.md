## What to expect

- Built on reka-ui's `Switch`, the same primitive shadcn-vue uses, so `v-model`, keyboard support, and the `switch` role behave the same.
- The thumb and track are goo, with motion tuned in the playground before it ships.
- Reduced motion makes the toggle instant and keeps the goo shape.
