## Planned behavior

- **Open:** a goo bridge stretches from the trigger to the menu and pinches off in about 250ms. Then the filter turns off.
- **Close:** the menu shrinks back toward the trigger, faster than it opened.
- **No goo on menu items.** Moving through items with the pointer or keyboard is never slowed down by goo.
- **Reduced motion:** the menu appears instantly or with a short fade.

It's built on reka-ui's `DropdownMenu`, so submenus, checkbox and radio items, typeahead, and keyboard navigation match shadcn-vue.
