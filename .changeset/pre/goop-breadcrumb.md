New component: GoopBreadcrumb (shadcn-vue's Breadcrumb with List, Item, Link, Page, Separator and Ellipsis). The links' hover / keyboard-focus highlight is one goo blob that flows from link to link with a calm feel and a low swell under the pointer (core `attachBreadcrumbHighlight`, the menu highlight fed `BREADCRUMB_FEEL`). Toggles: `highlight`, `wobble`, `momentum`, `followPointer`, plus `speed`, `preset`, `renderer` and `goo`.
