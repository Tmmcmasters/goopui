New `GoopCarousel` (shadcn-vue's Carousel on embla-carousel-vue, now a dependency of `@goopui/vue`): slides stretch with momentum and wobble on the snap (`stretch`), their edges slosh (`slosh`), a gooey rubber band past either end (`rubberBand`), goo `GoopCarouselDots` (`dots`), and Previous / Next squish (`squish`). Core: `attachCarousel`, `attachCarouselDots`, `CAROUSEL_FEEL`. Nuxt pre-bundles `embla-carousel-vue`.
