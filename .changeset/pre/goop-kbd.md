New component: GoopKbd and GoopKbdGroup (shadcn-vue's Kbd). Clicking a key, or pressing the keys in its opt-in `keys` prop (e.g. `['Meta', 'K']`), squishes it into its base like a soft key, with a small jelly rebound (core `attachKbd`). Toggles: `press`, plus `keys`, `speed`, `preset` and `goo`.
