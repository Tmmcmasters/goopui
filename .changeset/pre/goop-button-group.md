New component: GoopButtonGroup (shadcn-vue's ButtonGroup with Separator and Text). The buttons flex as one goo body: pressing one squishes it and tugs its neighbours toward it, with no gaps or seams (core `attachButtonGroup`). GoopButtons inside a group leave their own goo off unless they set `goo`. Toggles: `press`, `tug`, plus `speed`, `preset` and `goo`.
