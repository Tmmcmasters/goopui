goopUI: the puddle

The mark is one goopy Slime-green splat: a lumpy blob with a few lobes and two droplets flung off its edge (a lead drop and a speck), like a drop that just landed. It is not drawn by hand. It is the goop engine's own resting pose (core's SVG goo filter on a 52x38 round host plus six resting drips: four tucked in as lobes, two at the corners as droplets), traced to Beziers. The favicon keeps the body only. On the web the logo is live: apps/soon's GoopLogo.vue attaches the same engine with the same numbers, so the static SVG and the live goo are the same shape, and the blob wobbles toward the cursor, splashes droplets on fast passes and squishes on click. Reduced motion keeps this static mark. Wordmark: DynaPuff, ink = currentColor.
