goopUI brand · round 2

Round 2, set in DynaPuff SemiBold (OFL). Same construction as round 1: real path data, no filters; blobs and drips are signed distance fields joined with a C2 smooth-min and traced to cubic Béziers, and untouched letters are exact DynaPuff outlines. Marks are chunky enough that one cut serves 16px to 180px.

Pillow button

DynaPuff SemiBold

GoopButton as a pillow. A puffy, fully rounded button with a fat drip swelling off its bottom edge, the drip now big enough to read at header size. The body stays ink so it sits in a neutral shadcn UI; the slime drip is the only color. The wordmark is plain DynaPuff, so all the goo lives in the mark.

Merge

DynaPuff SemiBold

DynaPuff's own o's, pulled together. The second o bounces up a touch, the way DynaPuff's letters bob on the baseline, and a fat metaball bridge joins them. As a mark it's two chubby rings that read as oo, eyes, or a pair of bubbles; in the wordmark the fused oo is the only color.

Drip g

DynaPuff SemiBold

DynaPuff's g, with a fat drop hanging off the bottom of its hook, as if the descender is still running. (Swapping the hook for the drop turned the g into a q, so the hook stays.) Chunky enough to hold up as a one-letter favicon, and in the wordmark the drop is the only accent.

Melt

DynaPuff SemiBold

New for DynaPuff: the whole word is melting. Drips of different lengths hang off the o, the U and the I, so the wordmark looks warm and soft without changing a letter. The mark is the dripping U, a little cup of goo that also says UI.
