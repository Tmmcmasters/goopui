goopUI brand directions

Four directions, all real path data (no filters): blobs and drips are built as signed distance fields joined with a C2 smooth-min, so every join has continuous curvature, then traced into cubic Béziers. Text is outlined from OFL fonts. Favicon sizes use a slightly heavier small-size cut of each mark.

Button

Geist SemiBold

The product, literally. A shadcn-radius button with one drip hanging off its bottom edge, exactly the shape GoopButton makes on hover. The body is plain ink, so it sits quietly in a neutral UI, and the drip is the only color. Geist keeps the wordmark in the same family as the shadcn and Vercel world it lives next to.

Merge

Outfit SemiBold

The double o in goop, caught mid-merge. Two rings pulled together by a metaball bridge, the same goo the engine's filter makes when two shapes get close. It reads as oo, as a pair of eyes, as two things becoming one. Outfit's geometric bowls make the bridge in the wordmark feel native instead of stuck on.

Drip g

Bricolage Grotesque Bold

A monogram with a personality. A binocular g whose lower loop has stopped being a loop and is now a heavy drop hanging from the bowl. Bricolage Grotesque brings some ink-trap character to the wordmark, so this is the most editorial, illustrative option and the most ownable as a single letter.

Droplet

DM Sans SemiBold

One viscous drop necking off a ledge, the instant before it lets go. It is the simplest silhouette of the four and the easiest to spot in a row of tabs. In the wordmark the I drips the same drop below the baseline. DM Sans is friendly and neutral and lets the honey do the talking.
